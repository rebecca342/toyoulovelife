Module 1  |  Understanding Your VA Loan Benefit

What Is a VA Loan?

A VA loan is a mortgage loan guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. It is one of the most powerful home financing tools available — designed specifically to help veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses achieve homeownership.

Key Advantage No down payment required in most cases, no private mortgage insurance (PMI), and competitively low interest rates.

Who Is Eligible?

  • Veterans who served on active duty and were discharged under conditions other than dishonorable
  • Active-duty service members (after 90 continuous days of service)
  • National Guard & Reserve members (after 6 years of service or 90 days active duty)
  • Surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability

VA Loan Entitlement

Your entitlement is the dollar amount the VA will guarantee on your loan. Most veterans have full entitlement, which means no loan limit — you can borrow as much as a lender will approve without a down payment.

Module 2  |  Getting Ready to Buy

Pre-Purchase Checklist

  • Obtain your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the VA or through your lender
  • Check and understand your credit score (minimum varies by lender; many accept 580+)
  • Review your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio — aim for 41% or lower
  • Gather financial documents: last 2 years of tax returns, W-2s, recent pay stubs, bank statements
  • Get pre-approved by a VA-approved lender before shopping for homes
  • Determine your target budget and monthly payment comfort zone
  • Research neighborhoods, school districts, and commute times
  • Connect with a VA-experienced real estate agent

Module 3  |  The Home Buying Process — Step by Step

From Pre-Approval to Closing

  1. Get Pre-Approved — Submit your financial documents to a VA-approved lender and receive your pre-approval letter.
  2. Find a VA-Savvy Agent — Partner with a real estate agent experienced in VA transactions who will advocate for you.
  3. Shop for Homes — Identify homes that meet VA property requirements (MPRs). The home must be safe, sound, and sanitary.
  4. Make an Offer — Your agent submits an offer. You can negotiate seller concessions (up to 4%) to cover closing costs.
  5. Under Contract — The seller accepts. Now the clock starts on inspections, appraisal, and underwriting.
  6. VA Appraisal — A VA-assigned appraiser verifies the home meets MPRs and confirms fair market value.
  7. Underwriting & Approval — The lender reviews all documents and issues a Clear to Close (CTC).
  8. Closing Day — Sign the documents, pay any closing costs, and receive your keys. Congratulations — you're a homeowner!
VA Funding Fee Most VA loans require a funding fee (typically 1.25%–3.3% of the loan amount) paid at closing or rolled into the loan. Veterans with a service-connected disability rating of 10% or more are exempt.

Quick Reference  |  Key Terms Glossary
Amortization
The process of paying off a loan over time through regular monthly payments that cover both principal and interest.
Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
The true yearly cost of a loan, including interest and fees, expressed as a percentage.
Certificate of Eligibility (COE)
A document from the VA that confirms your eligibility for the VA home loan benefit.
Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
Your total monthly debt payments divided by your gross monthly income. A lower DTI means better loan terms.
Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
A good-faith deposit made when your offer is accepted, typically 1%–2% of the purchase price.
Escrow
A neutral third-party account that holds funds (taxes, insurance) until specific conditions are met at closing.
Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs)
VA standards ensuring a home is safe, structurally sound, and sanitary before a VA loan is approved.
Pre-Approval
A lender's conditional commitment to lend you a specific amount, based on a review of your financial profile.
VA Funding Fee
A one-time fee paid to the VA to help sustain the loan program. Waived for veterans with a service-connected disability.
VA Loan Entitlement
The amount the VA guarantees on your behalf. Full entitlement means no loan limits or down payment requirement.
