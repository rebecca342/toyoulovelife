What is Coffee & Contracts?

Coffee & Contracts is an all-in-one content marketing platform built specifically for real estate agents — focused on saving time, enhancing visibility, and driving consistent leads through done-for-you templates, educational resources, and proven strategy.

Founded by real estate agent Haley Ingram, who built her entire lead pipeline from Instagram — never from cold calls. Used by 5,000+ agents nationwide. Starting at $54/mo (or ~$45/mo annually).

Think of it as the content engine that powers everything else in your stack — it feeds Instagram, fills the link in bio, drives email signups, and keeps your brand consistent without hours of weekly effort.

Your Complete Tech Stack

1
Google Workspace Foundation — own everything
The foundation every other tool plugs into. Domain-based email (carmen@...), Google Drive as the shared asset library, Google Contacts as the master contact list. You own your data — leads, emails, and documents live in your account, not a vendor's platform. Set this up first.
2
Coffee & Contracts Content engine & lead magnets
Done-for-you Instagram content, monthly calendar, 113+ Reel templates, and a full library of buyer/seller guides ready to deploy as lead magnets. Brand the Canva templates once — then content takes ~2 hours/month. Starting at $54/mo.
3
Flodesk Email delivery & nurture sequences
Replace Mailchimp. Flat $38/month unlimited subscribers — Mailchimp costs more as your list grows. Natively integrates with every C&C lead magnet workflow. Beautiful landing pages already built. Someone submits → they automatically receive the guide. Connects to Google Workspace for data ownership.
4
Stan Store Link in bio hub & automations
The Instagram command center. Houses all lead magnets in one link in bio. Enables DM automation (reply to comments/DMs with freebie links), comment automation (e.g. "Comment GUIDE"), and calendar booking — all without leaving Instagram. Points leads to Flodesk landing pages.
5
Slack Real-time alerts & team comms
The nerve center — nothing falls through the cracks. Auto-alerts for every new lead, DM trigger, and campaign launch. Channels: #new-leads, #instagram-activity, #marketing-calendar, #listing-updates, #team-ops. Connected via Zapier (Flodesk → Slack, Stan Store → Slack).
6
Brivity CRM Active transactions & listings
The operational CRM — for leads in motion, active listings, showing follow-up, and under-contract communication. If they're in a transaction or close to one → Brivity. Receives leads from Flodesk via Zapier integration.
7
Buffini CRM Long-term relationships & referrals
The relationship CRM — past clients, sphere of influence, referral partners, monthly mailers, the "people who already love you" pipeline. Complements Brivity, doesn't compete. Google Workspace is the master contact list that bridges both systems.

Getting Started

Priority Action Plan

1

Set up Google Workspace

Get domain-based emails live. Migrate existing contacts to Google Contacts. Set up shared Drive for C&C templates, Canva exports, and listing assets. This is the ownership layer — everything else connects here.

2

Switch to Flodesk — migrate Mailchimp list

Export Mailchimp subscribers → import to Flodesk. Build 2–3 lead magnet workflows using C&C templates (Buyer Guide, Relocation Guide, Seller Guide). Landing pages are already designed — just brand them.

3

Brand the C&C templates once in Canva

Set up the Canva branding kit with W+AHT colors, fonts, and logo. Use bulk-export to brand the full 30-day content calendar at once. This is the one-time setup investment — after this, monthly content takes ~2 hours.

4

Deploy Stan Store link in bio

Point the Instagram bio link to Stan Store. Add Buyer Guide, Relocation Guide, and Seller Guide as the top 3 magnets — each pointing to the corresponding Flodesk landing page. Set up DM & comment automation for Reel CTAs.

5

Set up Slack + Zapier automations

Create channels: #new-leads, #instagram-activity, #marketing-calendar, #listing-updates, #team-ops. Connect Zapier: Flodesk new subscriber → #new-leads. Stan Store trigger → #instagram-activity. Never miss a lead.

6

Activate the monthly C&C content calendar

Use the C&C monthly calendar as the posting rhythm. Customize every post with Bellingham-specific details to stand out from national agents using the same templates. Personalization is the differentiator.

7

Establish the Brivity ↔ Buffini handoff rule

Active buyer/seller in a transaction → Brivity. Past client, referral partner, sphere of influence → Buffini. Google Contacts is the master list bridging both — no lead falls through the gap between the two systems.
