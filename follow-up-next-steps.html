2 Key Takeaways from Today's Class
  • Your VA benefit is powerful — no down payment, no PMI, and competitive rates make it one of the best loan products available.
  • Eligibility is the first step — obtain your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) early; your lender can often pull it directly.
  • Credit matters, but it doesn't have to be perfect — most VA-approved lenders accept scores of 580+; there are steps you can take now to improve yours.
  • Pre-approval is your power move — sellers take pre-approved buyers more seriously, giving you a competitive edge in any market.
  • Closing costs are manageable — you can negotiate seller concessions up to 4% to cover them, and the VA funding fee can be rolled into the loan.
  • The VA appraisal protects you — minimum property requirements ensure the home is safe, sound, and a fair value.
  • Working with the right team matters — choose a lender and real estate agent experienced in VA transactions.
